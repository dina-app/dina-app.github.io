DinaLab design system

Every public page now renders from a single stylesheet, assets/dinalab.css. Use the toggle in the nav to check both themes — the whole system is token-driven, so nothing is hard-coded to light.

Colour tokens

Surfaces are warm paper rather than neutral grey, which is what keeps the site reading as editorial instead of generic SaaS. Rust is the single brand accent; blue and green carry meaning (links and released status) and are never decorative.

Type scale

Every display size is fluid, so headings shrink with the viewport instead of stepping at a breakpoint. Japanese gets looser leading at h1 and h2, because tight Latin display leading collapses on CJK.
