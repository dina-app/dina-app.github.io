Operating principles

Business software should make control visible.

These principles shape every product decision, from browser permissions to release labels and documentation.

01

Credentials stay out of AI chat

Salesforce API tools execute in the extension. Session IDs and authorization headers are excluded from AI chat requests.

02

Review before consequential action

Write, deploy, update, and delete flows keep explicit review and confirmation steps visible.

03

Maturity is stated honestly

Released, next release, beta, prototype, and development preview are kept distinct.

04

Guidance is part of the product

Manuals, privacy disclosures, and practical business guides explain both operation and decision-making.
