Working principles

Make the important boundaries visible.

These rules connect product design, implementation, validation, and documentation.

  1. 01

    Useful before impressive

    Start with a real recurring task and solve the smallest complete part.

  2. 02

    Evidence over assumption

    Keep local, Sandbox, production, and public release proof distinct.

  3. 03

    People confirm consequential actions

    AI can advise, but write, deploy, update, and delete flows retain explicit review.

  4. 04

    Status stays honest

    Released, next release, beta, prototype, and development remain different states.
