    What happens to what you write

    Your messages are sent to OpenAI to write the answer and are not stored there. Feedback you confirm is kept by DinaLab with your name, Google email address and that conversation, so the team can follow up. Do not paste passwords, session IDs or customer data. To have your feedback deleted, ask in the chat.
